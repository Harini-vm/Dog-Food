How we score

Each review is turned into a number from 0 to 100 using the published rubric weights. Because some judges score everyone high and others everyone low, each judge's reviews are adjusted to the panel's average and spread, gently for judges with few reviews. A project's score is the average of its adjusted reviews plus one average review, so two lucky reviews do not beat five solid ones. Equal scores share a rank. Method: {{ snap.method }}.
